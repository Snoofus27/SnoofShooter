SnoofShooter

Tap shooters. Blast blocks. Build the picture.

Levels

How to play
  • Tap the front shooter in any column to send it to the dock.
  • Docked shooters fire at the lowest block in each column that matches their color.
  • The number is its ammo. At 0 it leaves and frees the slot.
  • Clear every block to build the picture. If all 5 slots fill up and nobody can shoot, you lose.
